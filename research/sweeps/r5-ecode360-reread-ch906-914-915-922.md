| 1 | SS-O §906.08 applies to natural slope ≥25% | **CONFIRMED** | §906.08.A.1: "a natural slope of twenty-five (25) percent or greater". |
| 2 | SS-O: Planning Commission review for all development | **CONFIRMED** | §906.08.C.1: "all uses and structures permitted in the base underlying district shall be reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission per the procedures of this Section." |
| 3 | SS-O: 21-day notice; hearing within 60 days; decision within 45 days | **CONFIRMED, with a CORRECTION on the default** | §906.08.C.3: at least 21 days' notice to owners within 150 ft (at least the 25 nearest owners). C.4: decision "within forty-five (45) days of the Planning Commission hearing". If the Commission misses either deadline, "the decision shall be deemed to be rendered in denial of the applicant unless the applicant has agreed in writing or on the record to an extension of time." **A missed deadline is a deemed *denial*, not an approval.** The KB does not say this. |
| 4 | SS-O: 50 ft setback from ridgeline or base | **CORRECTED (more precise)** | §906.08.C.5.6: "Development shall be set back fifty (50) feet **in both directions** from the edge of the SS-O boundary when it occurs at either the Ridgeline or Base." So the setback runs both ways, into and out of the overlay. Also: "The Planning Commission may waive any of the above standards if finds that other mitigating measures are taken", so the 50 ft setback **can be waived**. |
| 5 | SS-O: no outright ban | **CONFIRMED** | No prohibition in §906.08. It adds 14 review standards (C.5.1–14) on top of §915.02 and Chapter 916, and approval lapses after 1 year (C.7). |
| 6 | LS-O §906.04: triggered by excavation, fill or vegetation removal; field investigation by a registered professional; ZA plus Building Chief | **CONFIRMED + addition** | §906.04.B.2–3 match. **Addition:** §906.04.B.1 says every new or changed use and every structure erected or enlarged in LS-O "shall comply with the Hillside Development Standards of the Subdivision Regulations", and requires a development plan for the Certificate of Occupancy. B.3 cites "Section 906.03.B.2", which appears to be a stale cross-reference in the code. |
| 7 | UM-O §906.05: single-unit dwelling allowed with >100 ft overburden and no subsidence history | **CONFIRMED** | §906.05.B.2: "more than one hundred (100) feet of overburden above the mine and that there is no known history of subsidence incidents in the vicinity". It covers single-unit use "or another structure that is comparable in size and construction characteristics". |
| 8 | UM-O: larger or heavier buildings, or <100 ft overburden, prohibited until a site investigation shows it is safe | **CONFIRMED** | §906.05.B.3 "Other Development Prohibited. Until the applicant has submitted evidence that the site is reasonably safe…". Applies to <100 ft overburden, any subsidence history, "any structure that is larger or heavier than a typical single-unit dwelling", or hazardous uses. So **any 2+ unit building larger than a typical single-unit house needs a site investigation**. B.1 also requires DEP mine data for all new construction or enlargement. |
| 9 | FP-O floodway: no new construction without a no-rise hydraulic analysis AND a DEP permit | **CONFIRMED** | §906.02.E.2.(a)(i): no encroachment "unless it has been demonstrated through hydrologic and hydraulic analysis … that the proposed encroachment would not result in any increase in flood levels". (a)(ii): "no new construction or development shall be allowed, unless the appropriate permit is obtained from the Department of Environmental Protection Regional Office." §906.02.I.1(a) and §922.09.F.4 bar variances that would increase flood levels in the floodway. |
| 10 | FP-O: lowest floor at or above the regulatory flood elevation | **CONFIRMED + precision** | §906.02.F.2(a)(1). The regulatory flood elevation is defined (J.2(aa)) as BFE "plus a freeboard safety factor of one and one-half (1½) feet". So the effective rule is **BFE + 1.5 ft**. |
| 11 | FP-O decider: Zoning plus a PLI Floodplain Permit | **CONFIRMED (code wording differs)** | §906.02.B requires "zoning approval … from the Zoning Administrator and a permit … issued by the Bureau of Building Inspection". The code does not use the name "Floodplain Permit"; that is the OneStopPGH record type. |
| 12 | FP-O trigger: FEMA A/AE zone | **CONFIRMED + addition** | §906.02.E.1 adopts the FIS/FIRMs "dated September 26, 2014 … or the most recent revision thereof", **plus** "Community Identified Flood Hazard Areas" on a "Local Flood Hazard Map". This partly answers the KB question about FEMA 2014: 2014 is the base, with later revisions adopted automatically. |

| 23 | §922.04: ZA decides; may forward to PC within 14 days | **CONFIRMED + detail** | C.1: 14 days. D: the PC role is "advisory only". PC has 60 days, and silence = "deemed to be a recommendation for approval". C.2: ZA acts within 21 days of the PC recommendation. F: notice of decision within 5 business days. H: the site plan expires after 1 year without construction. No overall ZA deadline when the ZA keeps the file. |
| 24 | §922.06 CU: PC recommends within 45 days; Council hears within 45 days of PC and decides; 7 votes if PC recommends denial | **CONFIRMED + precision** | C: PC recommendation "within forty-five (45) days of the Commission hearing", and the PC hearing must be held within 45 days of a complete application. D.1: Council hearing within 45 days of PC action, and decision within 45 days of the Council hearing. F: "no fewer than seven (7) members". Missed deadlines at PC or Council = **deemed denial**. Notice: 21 days, 150 ft radius, at least 25 notices. Criteria: seven "no detrimental impact" tests (E.1(a)–(g)). |
| 25 | §922.07 SE: ZBA; 21-day notice; decision within 45 days | **CONFIRMED + precision** | B: at least 21 days' notice to abutting owners, owners across the street, and Council members. C: hearing within 45 days of a complete application, and decision within 45 days of the hearing. Missed deadline = deemed denial. Criteria: the same seven tests as CU (D.1(a)–(g)). The KB's "decision within 45 days after the record closes" comes from the ZBA handout; the code says "within forty-five (45) days of the Board hearing". |
| 26 | §922.08 Administrator Exception: decision due 21 days after a complete application | **CONFIRMED + correction on the default** | C: "Within twenty-one (21) days of receipt of a complete application". A miss = "deemed to have been rendered in denial", and may go "to the Zoning Board of Adjustment for immediate review". The applicant posts notice *after* approval (B). |
| 27 | §922.09 Variance: ZBA, any dimensional or use noncompliance | **CONFIRMED + criteria** | E: all five findings required: (1) unique physical circumstances, (2) "no possibility that the property can be developed in strict conformity", (3) hardship not self-created, (4) no change to essential character, (5) minimum variance. Notice: 21 days, at least the 6 nearest owners plus abutting and across-the-street owners. Decision within 45 days of the hearing; a miss = deemed denial. |
| 28 | §922.10 PDP applicability "District-specific (RIV, GT, UC, SP, LNC bonus, etc.)" | **CONFIRMED** | B: "In each applicable district … developments shall be in accord with a Project Development Plan approved by the Commission." §922.10 itself has no project-size trigger. | 30 | ZBA fee $400 not in the code | **CONFIRMED (fine)** | §922.07 and §922.09 refer only to "a nonrefundable fee that has been established". No dollar amount is in the code. The $400 stays sourced to the ZBA handout. |
| 31 | Durations "+2–4 months" for SS-O, "~3–5 months" for CU | **Not code claims** | The code gives maximum statutory windows only. ## Summary

- **Confirmed (24 claims):** SS-O ≥25% trigger with PC review for all uses, and its 21/60/45-day windows. LS-O and UM-O mechanics, including the >100 ft overburden allowance for single-unit homes. The FP-O floodway no-rise + DEP permit rule. The §915.02 tree survey >¼ acre, cut/fill >25% geotech + terracing, retaining walls ≤10 ft. §922.04 triggers (≥4 units, H district, lot-size and parking triggers). CU, SE, AE and variance deciders and deadlines. The CU 7-vote rule. PDP applicability as district-specific. ZBA fee not in the code. All Parking Schedule A residential rows, including the disputed single-unit-attached max of 4. All §914.04 reductions and exclusions.
- **Corrected or sharpened (7):** (1) Missed deadlines for SS-O, CU, SE, variance and AE are **deemed denials**. (2) The SS-O 50 ft setback runs "in both directions" from the SS-O edge and **the PC may waive it**. (3) FP-O elevation is effectively **BFE + 1.5 ft**. (4) Tree replacement is **total diameter**, measured at 4 ft. (5) "Table 914.02.A" is **"Parking Schedule A"**. (6) A parking shortfall goes to an **Alternative Access and Parking Plan**, decided by the ZA when ≤10 spaces are required and by ZBA Special Exception when >10, not automatically a variance. (7) §911.02: **GT sits under the DT group header**; the column order itself is right.
- **Not found:** the "40% slope no-disturbance" rule (not in §906.08 or §915.01–.02; the whole code was not searched). The PDP "≥15,000 sf GFA / ≥40 structured spaces" threshold (not in §922.10).
- **Not checked:** the §914.05 bicycle-parking 30% swap. The Hillside Development Standards of the Subdivision Regulations, which LS-O requires.
