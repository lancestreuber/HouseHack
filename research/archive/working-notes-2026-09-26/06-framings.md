# Alternative framings (Track 1)

These are options and tradeoffs, not a pick. Several could share one parcel/zoning rules engine.

| # | Framing | User / moment | Decision changed | Where the LLM adds value | Main risk | Feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parcel scorecard + citywide map (literal brief) | Planner, developer | Which parcels to pursue or fast-track | Plain-language explanation with citations | Likely similar to other entries; the "site selection" pain is weakly evidenced (see 04) | High |
| 2 | Land Bank / city-lot disposition prioritizer | Land Bank / URA staff; CDCs requesting lots | Which of the lots to clear and market first, and for what use | Per-lot rationale, board memo draft | Their priorities (funding, community goals) may not be "ease"; messy inventory data | High |
| 3 | Assemblage finder | CDC / mission-driven developer acquisitions | Combine small or steep lots into a viable site | Explaining threshold crossings; drafting outreach | Geometry bugs; owner-contact privacy | Medium |
| 4 | Starter-home / missing-middle "what can I build" | Small builders, homeowners with side lots | Whether to buy or build, and which typology | Answers in plain language quoting the code | Legal-advice-shaped tool while the code is being rewritten | Medium-high |
| 5 | Pre-application / variance packet drafter with ZBA precedents | Applicant before filing | Redesign to stay by right vs file for relief; fewer resubmissions | High: precedent retrieval, drafting, completeness checklist (matches the Mayor's stated AI interest) | ZBA PDFs are hard to fetch (CDN blocks); could be seen as "helping get around zoning" | Medium |
| 6 | Planner intake triage queue | City zoning intake staff | Routing: fast-track vs hearing | Summarizing applications against the parcel | No real application documents available (synthetic demo); unclear city demand | Medium |
| 7 | Policy "unlock" simulator | Council staff, Planning, advocates | Which amendment to push | Turning amendment text into rule parameters, with human review | Code complexity; politically loaded counts | Medium |
| 8 | Rehab / adaptive-reuse ease | Land Bank rehab pilot, small rehab investors | Rehab vs demolish vs build | Summarizing violation histories | Building condition data isn't public | Medium-low |
| 9 | Subsidy stack + gap matcher ("Pro Forma" half) | Affordable developer finance lead | Does it pencil, and which programs to apply to | QAP criteria with page citations | Cost and rent inputs are guesses; must show ranges | Medium |
