# Open questions for mentors (Slack office hours, Sat/Sun 10 a.m.–6 p.m. ET)

## Who and what
1. Who should the primary user be: Land Bank or URA staff, CDCs, small builders, or City planners? Are any of them sponsors or judges?
2. Where do Pittsburgh housing projects actually stall the most: ZBA and variances, RCO and Planning Commission, title clearing, or financing?
3. Would the City or the Land Bank actually use a tool that ranks specific lots, or is that politically risky? Would an analyst- or advocate-facing tool be a safer fit?
4. For the County, would a municipality-level "zoning barrier index" be more useful than a parcel score?
5. How should we weigh rehab of vacant structures against new construction on vacant lots?

## Rules and data
6. Should we score against current zoning, Bill 2025-1545 as recommended, the Phase I amendment (Bill 2026-0834), or several of these as scenarios? Has 2025-1545 had a Council vote since the 9/23 hearing?
7. Is there a machine-readable version of the zoning code? Is structured ZBA decision data available?
8. What exactly are the "approved affordability assumptions" behind the Pro Forma Navigator? Is a financing pro forma in scope for Track 1?
9. Is there any internal data on acquisition status (treasurer or sheriff sale pipeline, conservatorship) that we could use or mock?
10. Is the City's "AI for missing info" completeness tool procured or in pilot? Would our tool conflict with it or complement it?

## Scope and judging
11. The submission form asks for **one** track. How will judges treat an entry that combines Track 1 and Track 3? Which track should we list?
12. Would judges penalize a tool that covers only the City of Pittsburgh (not all 130 municipalities) if we state it clearly as a limitation?
13. Can we reach Dr. Evaine Sing (PHFA fellow working on vacant lots) or Pro-Housing Pittsburgh for a quick check that we're on the right track?
