## The five facts most likely to change a decision

1. Building starts Sat Sept 26, 9:00 a.m. ET and submissions close Sun Sept 27, 11:59 p.m. ET, with no extensions ([rules](rules-and-deliverables.md)).
2. The form takes **one** track ([rules](rules-and-deliverables.md)).
3. The limitations statement is a **mandatory** deliverable, and the packet says admitting "We don't have good data on X" is scored as a strength ([brief and judging](brief-and-judging.md)).
4. Judges watch the demo video first ([rules](rules-and-deliverables.md)).
5. The Track 1 brief body asks for a Development Ease Score and never mentions a pro forma, even though the landing page and brief title say "Pro Forma Navigator" ([brief and judging](brief-and-judging.md)).
