## The finding that matters most

**No stakeholder names "which site is easiest" as their constraint.** They name acquisition and title, the financing and appraisal gap, preservation, IZ cost uncertainty, zoning and variances, and Land Bank funding. See [practitioners](practitioners.md). Any Track 1 framing has to answer that.

## Where stakeholders disagree

- **City vs. County:** growth vs. preservation; one city vs. many municipalities.
- **IZ:** CDCs supported mandatory IZ; Pro-Housing Pittsburgh and the building trades opposed it. Council moved to an optional bonus. See [inclusionary zoning and bonus](../policy/inclusionary-zoning-and-bonus.md).
- **Rehab cost:** ACTION-Housing's CEO says rehab often costs more per square foot than new construction; the Land Bank's inventory is mostly rehab.
