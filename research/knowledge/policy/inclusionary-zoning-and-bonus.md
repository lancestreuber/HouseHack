# Inclusionary zoning and the Affordable Housing Bonus

**Type:** policy
**One line:** The mandatory Inclusionary Housing Overlay (IZ-O) that applies today in four neighborhoods, and the two competing voluntary affordable-housing texts in pending Bill 2025-1545 *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)*.
**Why we care:** IZ is a set-aside cost on 20+ unit projects inside the overlay, and practitioners cite it as a reason projects pause. The bonus would add height in exchange for affordable units. Both belong in the pro forma as configurable toggles.
**Last checked:** 2026-09-26

## Current: Inclusionary Housing Overlay (IZ-O)

| Term | Value | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Lawrenceville, Bloomfield, Polish Hill, Oakland | GIS `[read]` |
| Applies to | Projects of **20+ units** | `[skimmed]` (PublicSource / Pro-Housing Pittsburgh) |
| Set-aside | **10%** of units | `[skimmed]` |
| Affordability | Rents affordable at **50% AMI** | `[skimmed]` |
| Term | **35 years**, rental and owner units | EngagePgh IZ page `[read]` |

- The overlay is live in the City GIS: `InclusionaryHousingOverlayDistrict` (1 feature), and "IZ-O" appears in the combined `PGHWebZoningOverlays` layer `[read]` ([r1 zoning sweep](../../sweeps/r1-zoning-data-code-and-reforms.md)).
- The 20-unit, 10% and 50% AMI terms come from secondary sources only; they have not been read in the code text. Under the domain rule they stay `[skimmed]`.
- *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* One ZBA decision is consistent with them: a **159-unit** R-MU project in Central Oakland asked for a §907.04 waiver from "16 affordable units req / 0 prop" and was **denied** (Feb 2026) `[read]` ([ZBA decisions](../data/zba-decisions.md)). 16/159 ≈ 10% (our arithmetic; the rounding rule is not in the decision).

## Proposed: two competing voluntary texts in Bill 2025-1545 *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)*

The Planning Commission's Report & Recommendation (letter June 11, 2026) sets out both `[read]` ([saved text](../../sources/pittsburgh-legistar-2026-09-26-zoning-bills-1545-0834-1579.md), [sweep](../../sweeps/r5-council-records-and-methodologies.md)):

| | **Council committee substitute (Oct 15, 2025)** | **PC-recommended substitute (June 2, 2026)** |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | Voluntary **Affordable Housing Bonus Program**, "except in areas in the existing Inclusionary Zoning Overlay (IZ-O)" | Extend the **existing §915.07 Affordable Housing Performance Points** citywide |
| Where | citywide outside IZ-O | adds RM, NDO, LNC, NDI, UNC, UI and select GPR subdistricts; updates RIV, UPR, UC-MU, UC-E, R-MU |
| Threshold / set-aside | ≥20 units; 10% affordable | points scale with affordability depth |
| Affordability | rentals ≤50% AMI for 20 years; for-sale ≤80% AMI, resale +2%/yr | 20 years for the voluntary program (PC condition 2) |
| Bonus | up to **+30 ft height and +2 FAR** (except projects already eligible for §915.07 points) | points → height ("each point equates to 15 feet", critique's text extraction `[skimmed]`) |
| Alternatives | off-site at 12%; payment in lieu **$25/sf** | on-site, off-site and payment in lieu |
| Other | City Planning reports to Council within 2 years | PC condition 1: payment-in-lieu amounts set annually in the fee schedule, not in the code |

- ADUs and **removal of parking minimums** are in both substitutes.
- PC condition 2: "the mandatory program stays unchanged" (inference: IZ-O stays).
- **Neither is law.** Bill 2025-1545 is Held In Council, not on the 9/29–9/30/26 agendas, and needs a committee recommendation, a Council vote (5 votes) and the Mayor's signature. Which text Council adopts is undecided.

Earlier reading, from the June 2026 draft on the EngagePgh IZ page `[read]`:

- Applies where **4+ units are allowed**, excluding the Golden Triangle zoning district.
- **10–20%** of units affordable: at or below **50% AMI for rentals** and **80% AMI for owner units**, for **20 years**.
- Bonus: **1 point = 15 ft of extra height**, 2–6 points; or a **payment in lieu of $25 per sq ft**. The June 2, 2026 PC redline on Legistar builds the bonus on §915.07 Performance Points and says "each point equates to 15 feet" (critique's text extraction, 2026-09-26).
- This is a pending bill, not the code. Claims here are about the bill's draft, not current law. ⚠ The EngagePgh "4+ units" and "10–20%" terms do not obviously match either substitute as described in the PC R&R; the PC R&R is the more authoritative, later record *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)*.

## How it got here: mandatory citywide IZ became an optional bonus

- Bill 2025-1545 originally proposed citywide mandatory inclusionary zoning alongside ADUs and parking reform. At the September 2025 Council hearing (⚠ Legistar dates it **9/10/25**, not Sept 11 *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 9)*), CDCs (Lawrenceville United, OPDC) supported IZ; Pro-Housing Pittsburgh and the building trades opposed it as an unfunded cost `[read]` (citizenportal.ai summary, a third-party summary not checked against the primary record).
- ⚠ On **10/15/25** Legistar records **"AMENDED BY SUBSTITUTE" in Standing Committees**, plus referral to the Planning Commission. That is a committee action, **not a full-Council vote** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 9)*. WESA reported a **5–4** vote to rewrite the proposal into an optional bonus ([WESA 2025-10-15](https://www.wesa.fm/politics-government/2025-10-15/inclusionary-zoning-debate-rages-on-as-pittsburgh-city-council-votes-to-rewrite-proposed-policy)). *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The **5 Aye – 4 No** tally is now **verified** in the Legistar vote record and the 10/15/25 Standing Committee minutes `[read]`. The substitute "kept the provisions for Accessory Dwelling Units and Parking Reform but replaced the City-wide Inclusionary Zoning provisions with an Affordable Housing Bonus program" (PC finding 5).
- The alternative-framings sweep summarizes the arc as "moved from a mandatory policy to a voluntary bonus", with a June 2026 draft still under revision `[read]` ([WESA 2025-09-12](https://www.wesa.fm/politics-government/2025-09-12/pittsburgh-inclusionary-zoning-debates-housing-affordability) for the debate; [EngagePgh IZ page](https://engage.pittsburghpa.gov/implementing-housing-needs-assessment/inclusionary-zoning-iz) for the June 2026 draft).
- Planning Commission recommended the bill (June 2, 2026), but **with a different substitute** that extends Performance Points citywide rather than the Council's bonus *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)*. ⚠ Legistar (checked 2026-09-26): **Held In Council**; public hearings 9/10/25 and 9/23/26; committee substitute and PC referral 10/15/25; PC report received 6/12/26; **no final vote** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 9)*. See [reforms in flux](reforms-in-flux-2025-2026.md).
- The Mayor has reportedly said citywide inclusionary zoning is "off the table" `[skimmed]` (search snippet only).

**Contradiction (date of PC recommendation):** the approval and stakeholder sweeps give **June 2, 2026**; WESA's article is dated June 3, 2026 and the prior-art sweep says the PC recommended the voluntary version "on June 3". *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The PC's own decision page says **June 2, 2026** `[read]`, but its letter says the hearing was "June 5, 2026", an inconsistency inside the primary attachment. We use June 2 (decision page); June 3 is WESA's article date.

## Implications (inference)

- Model IZ-O as a hard rule for 20+ units inside the overlay, and the bonus as a scenario toggle.
- The bonus is height-denominated, so it interacts with the RM height limits in [dimensional standards](dimensional-standards-and-use-table.md).
- IZ-O's 35-year term vs. the bonus's 20-year term matter for a pro forma's affordability period.

## Open questions

- Read the IZ-O text in eCode360: unit threshold, set-aside, AMI level, and whether owner units differ.
- Which substitute Council adopts, and when it takes a final vote (Held In Council as of 2026-09-24 per Legistar; not on the 9/29–9/30/26 agendas).
- Would the bonus replace IZ-O in the four neighborhoods or sit alongside it? ⚠ **Primary texts conflict** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 25)*. *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* Legistar v1 would have **removed** IZ-O; the v2 title (Council substitute) **adds a sunset clause**; the Council bonus applies "except in areas in the existing IZ-O"; the PC redline amends §907.04.A7 without "sunset" text, and PC condition 2 says "the mandatory program stays unchanged" `[read]`. Best reading (inference): sunset under the Council text, IZ-O retained under the PC text. The sunset date/terms have not been read.
- Which AMI table (HUD FY2026) and rent-affordability convention does the City use? See [market and affordability](../data/market-and-affordability.md).
