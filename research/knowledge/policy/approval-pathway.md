# Approval pathway

**Type:** policy
**One line:** Every approval step a Pittsburgh housing project can hit, what triggers it, who decides, how long it takes and what it costs, with a verification tag per row.
**Why we care:** "Ease" is mostly about which discretionary steps a parcel-plus-project triggers. This table is the rules engine's spec.
**Last checked:** 2026-09-26

This is working research, not legal advice. Confirm with City Planning or mentors.

⚠ **Tag caveat:** the §922, §906, §914 and §915 code text in this node was read on the **zoneomics.com mirror** because eCode360, elaws and Municode blocked or timed out for the sweep. Per the domain rule, those code rules are **`[skimmed]`** even though the sweep marked them verified. The ZBA and Planning Commission process handouts are official dated City documents and are `[read]`.

## ZBA specifics

- From the ZBA process handout (Dec 2024) `[read]`: $400 fee, ≥21-day posted notice, hearings on the first 3 Thursdays of each month, decision within 45 days after the record closes, **appeal to Common Pleas within 30 days**, **approval expires in 1 year**.
- One parsed decision (1137 Pennsylvania Ave) had 44 days between hearing and decision `[read]`. Decision PDFs carry labeled fields (hearing date, decision date, zone case, address, lot/block, district, request, **BDA application number**, variance/SE table by section, decision paragraph) and join to OneStopPGH via the BDA number. See [ZBA decisions data](../data/zba-decisions.md).
- **Volume:** 210 ZBA applications in 2020 (Lenze, Hinojos and Grady 2024) `[read]`; the r2 sweep's own estimate is ~150–200 a year from ~5 cases per meeting `[unverified estimate]`.
- **Approval rates: none published** anywhere the sweeps searched. PublicSource Board Explorer shows members and cadence only `[read]`.
- **Contradiction:** the r1 prior-art sweep read Lenze et al. 2024 (210 applications from 2020, no significant overall relationship with a social vulnerability index, but significant links with the share of pre-1940 housing and of multifamily units). The r2 sweep reported it **could not find** that paper. We keep the r1 read; the r2 miss is a search failure, not a contrary finding.

## How a rules engine should use this (inference)

- Map parcel attributes (district, overlays, slope, flood, historic, lot size) plus project attributes (units, GFA, parking, disturbance) to the set of triggered rows.
- If the target unit count exceeds the district's by-right ceiling, or any dimensional or [parking](parking.md) minimum isn't met, output "Variance / ZBA".
- The ≥4-unit threshold appears three times (Site Plan Review, RCO DAM, PWSA multi-unit), so 3→4 units is a step change in process.
- Pin a code version: several rows may change with [pending reforms](reforms-in-flux-2025-2026.md), including who runs community meetings.
