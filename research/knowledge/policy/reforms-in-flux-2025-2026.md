**Why we care:** The code is being rewritten while we build. A score hard-coded to today's rules goes stale within weeks; the engine should pin a code version and support "current" vs. "if bill X passes" scenarios.

## ADUs under Bill 2025-1545

- ⚠ **Stale source** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 26)*. The EngagePgh ADU page (dating from late 2024) proposes ADUs by right, **up to 2 per lot, 1,000 sq ft maximum, no owner-occupancy requirement** `[read]`. Those are 2024 terms, and a primary text now exists.
- **Current ADU terms: see the June 2, 2026 PC redline of Bill 2025-1545 on Legistar.** The critique's text extraction of that redline shows: 1,000 sf; permitted on lots whose primary use is Residential, Community Center or Religious Assembly; 30 ft; exempt from Ch. 916 `[skimmed]` (text extraction only).
- *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The PC's own **Findings of Fact** (June 2, 2026) answer the per-lot count `[read]`: ADUs by right "on any lot where a primary use is a residential use, Community Center use or Religious Assembly use"; **a maximum of two ADUs per zoning lot containing a residential use**, each "up to two stories and 30 feet in height"; no limit on count for community-center or religious-assembly lots, with Site Plan Review above 10 units. The PC-recommended substitute **keeps 30 ft but removes the story limit**. Still unresolved from text extraction: the 1,000 sf cap and whether any owner-occupancy line survives.
- One sweep could not confirm that PC recommended citywide ADUs in June 2026; two other sweeps confirm the PC recommended Bill 2025-1545, which includes ADUs, on June 2.

## Contradictions kept visible

1. **Did the March plan have two phases or three?** The zoning and stakeholder sweeps (City release and WESA) describe **two phases**. The approval sweep, citing WESA, says **3 phases** and 20+ Phase I actions `[skimmed]`. *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The Bill 2026-0834 hearing report (July 28, 2026) `[read]` describes **three zoning-reform phases**: this Phase One amendment, then "a comprehensive overhaul of the Zoning Code", then "adopting the new Zoning Code and implementing a system for regular maintenance". So the zoning track has three phases in the City's own document; whether the March permitting plan counted two is a framing difference. Partly resolved.
2. **Does EO 2026-01 touch zoning?** A snippet of WESA (2026-01-07) has the Mayor saying the order does **not** touch planning, zoning or community-input steps `[skimmed]`. But the March plan that followed changes who runs community meetings, and the prior-art sweep reports the EngagePgh zoning amendment hub linking EO 2026-01 to a "Phase One" zoning amendment `[read]`. Likely reading (inference): the EO itself was a permitting review, and the zoning pieces came later through the March plan and Bill 2026-0834. *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The EO number is now `[read]` in City documents: the 2026-0834 hearing report says "On January 6, 2026 [the Mayor] introduced Executive Order No. 2026-01 to add predictability and efficiency to the City's permitting system", and the cover letter calls the bill "the first phase of the Mayor's Executive Order No. 2026-01". The City itself links the zoning amendment to the EO; whether the EO text mentions zoning is still unread.
3. **Is the 2050 comprehensive plan alive?** The March 2026 plan ties Phase II's code rewrite to a ~$6M 2050 comprehensive plan `[read]`. But WESA reported in Dec 2025 that Council moved to **freeze the plan's consultant contracts** `[skimmed]`. Growth scenarios had been scheduled for Aug 2025–Apr 2026, and no published scenario or typology maps were found. Whether the freeze was lifted, or Phase II proceeds without those contracts, is unknown. Pro-Housing Pittsburgh criticized the draft for labeling about half the city low- or no-growth `[skimmed]`.
4. **RCO meetings: announced change vs. current rule.** The March plan says the City will schedule its own public-input meetings instead of RCOs. The DCP Development Activities Meeting page (updated 5/5/26) still describes RCO-hosted meetings, and no ordinance change was found. We treat the RCO rule as current until a code change is found. See [approval pathway](approval-pathway.md).
5. **Citywide IZ.** A snippet has the Mayor calling citywide inclusionary zoning "off the table" `[skimmed]`, consistent with the Oct 2025 committee substitute that moved to an optional bonus (⚠ a Standing Committee action, not a full-Council vote *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 9)*), but the bonus itself is not enacted. See [inclusionary zoning and bonus](inclusionary-zoning-and-bonus.md).
6. ⚠ **Does Bill 2025-1545 sunset IZ-O?** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 25)* Primary texts conflict. *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* Legistar versions: **v1** would have **removed** IZ-O; the **v2** title (the Oct 2025 committee substitute) instead **adds a sunset clause** to IZ-O `[read]`. The June 2, 2026 PC redline amends §907.04.A7 (off-site inclusionary standards), the critique's search found no "sunset" text in it, and the PC's condition 2 says "the mandatory program stays unchanged" `[read]`. Best reading (inference): the sunset belongs to the Council substitute; the PC-recommended substitute leaves IZ-O in place. Which text Council adopts is undecided (contradiction 7).
7. **Which affordable-housing text will Council adopt?** *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)* The Oct 2025 Council substitute (voluntary bonus: ≥20 units, 10% affordable, rentals ≤50% AMI for 20 years, for-sale ≤80% AMI, +30 ft and +2 FAR, 12% off-site, $25/sf in lieu, outside IZ-O) or the June 2026 PC substitute (extend §915.07 Affordable Housing Performance Points to RM, NDO, LNC, NDI, UNC, UI and select GPR subdistricts, with on-site, off-site and payment-in-lieu options). The record does not decide it. See [inclusionary zoning and bonus](inclusionary-zoning-and-bonus.md).
8. **PC hearing date for 2025-1545:** the PC letter says "June 5, 2026"; its decision page says June 2, 2026. Internal inconsistency in the attachment; we use June 2.

## Implications (inference)

- Encode rules as data with a version tag: `code@2026-09-26` vs. `+2025-1545` vs. `+2026-0834`.
- If 2025-1545 passes: parking minimums drop out of the AAPP/ZBA triggers (both substitutes remove them per the PC findings `[read]` *(updated 2026-09-26, round 5)*); up to two ADUs per residential lot become by right; the affordable-housing piece is either a +30 ft / +2 FAR bonus or citywide Performance Points depending on which substitute is adopted. Model it as a scenario toggle ("if enacted as PC-recommended"), not current law.
- If 2026-0834 passes: re-read §903.03 and height rules; the [dimensional table](dimensional-standards-and-use-table.md) may change. ⚠ It also amends Ch. 906 overlays, Ch. 921 nonconformities and Ch. 922 review procedures, which are exactly the gate, lot-of-record and pathway rules the engine would encode *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 8)*.
