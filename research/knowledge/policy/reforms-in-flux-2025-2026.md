# Reforms in flux, 2025–2026

**Type:** policy
**One line:** Every Pittsburgh zoning and permitting change that is enacted, pending, or announced as of 2026-09-26, with its status and the contradictions between our sources.
**Why we care:** The code is being rewritten while we build. A score hard-coded to today's rules goes stale within weeks; the engine should pin a code version and support "current" vs. "if bill X passes" scenarios.

## Contradictions kept visible

1. **Did the March plan have two phases or three?** The zoning and stakeholder sweeps (City release and WESA) describe **two phases**. The approval sweep, citing WESA, says **3 phases** and 20+ Phase I actions `[skimmed]`. We use two (Phase I near-term, Phase II code rewrite) because the City's own release was read; a third phase may exist in WESA's reporting. Unresolved.
2. **Does EO 2026-01 touch zoning?** A snippet of WESA (2026-01-07) has the Mayor saying the order does **not** touch planning, zoning or community-input steps `[skimmed]`. But the March plan that followed changes who runs community meetings, and the prior-art sweep reports the EngagePgh zoning amendment hub linking EO 2026-01 to a "Phase One" zoning amendment `[read]`. Likely reading (inference): the EO itself was a permitting review, and the zoning pieces came later through the March plan and Bill 2026-0834. The EO number itself is `[skimmed]` in one sweep and `[read]` via the hub in another.
3. **Is the 2050 comprehensive plan alive?** The March 2026 plan ties Phase II's code rewrite to a ~$6M 2050 comprehensive plan `[read]`. But WESA reported in Dec 2025 that Council moved to **freeze the plan's consultant contracts** `[skimmed]`. Growth scenarios had been scheduled for Aug 2025–Apr 2026, and no published scenario or typology maps were found. Whether the freeze was lifted, or Phase II proceeds without those contracts, is unknown. Pro-Housing Pittsburgh criticized the draft for labeling about half the city low- or no-growth `[skimmed]`.
4. **RCO meetings: announced change vs. current rule.** The March plan says the City will schedule its own public-input meetings instead of RCOs. The DCP Development Activities Meeting page (updated 5/5/26) still describes RCO-hosted meetings, and no ordinance change was found. We treat the RCO rule as current until a code change is found. See [approval pathway](approval-pathway.md).
5. **Citywide IZ.** A snippet has the Mayor calling citywide inclusionary zoning "off the table" `[skimmed]`, consistent with the Oct 2025 committee substitute that moved to an optional bonus (⚠ a Standing Committee action, not a full-Council vote *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 9)*), but the bonus itself is not enacted. See [inclusionary zoning and bonus](inclusionary-zoning-and-bonus.md).
6. ⚠ **Does Bill 2025-1545 sunset IZ-O?** *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 25)* Primary texts conflict. The Legistar matter title says the bill would "add a sunset clause to 907.04.A IZ-O". The June 2, 2026 PC redline (Legistar attachment) amends §907.04.A7 (off-site inclusionary standards), and the critique's search found no "sunset" text in it, so the title may describe the original bill. Resolve from the substitute text before claiming either.

## Implications (inference)

- Encode rules as data with a version tag: `code@2026-09-26` vs. `+2025-1545` vs. `+2026-0834`.
- If 2025-1545 passes: parking minimums drop out of variance triggers; ADUs become by right; the bonus becomes a height toggle. (Whether the redline's parking minimums are struck is undetermined from text extraction.)
- If 2026-0834 passes: re-read §903.03 and height rules; the [dimensional table](dimensional-standards-and-use-table.md) may change. ⚠ It also amends Ch. 906 overlays, Ch. 921 nonconformities and Ch. 922 review procedures, which are exactly the gate, lot-of-record and pathway rules the engine would encode *(corrected 2026-09-26 per docs/04-critique.md row 8)*.
