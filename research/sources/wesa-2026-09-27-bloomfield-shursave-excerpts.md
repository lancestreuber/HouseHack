**WESA 2021-11-02** (https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2021-11-02/a-giant-eagle-apartments-and-retail-are-planned-for-a-crucial-corner-in-bloomfield): "'Bloomfield Square' would have underground parking, several small retail shops, a public plaza, and roughly 190 apartments, 10 percent of which would be affordable." / "its intent was quite narrow: to win community support for a zoning change on a small piece of land toward the back of the site. Currently, the land only allows residential housing, which Echo would like to switch to local neighborhood commercial, or LNC."

**WESA 2023-07-12** (https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2023-07-12/bloomfield-shursave-development-city-approval-process): "248 apartments above" / "25 of Echo's proposed apartments would be affordable at roughly half of the going rate" / "six studio apartments, 13 one-bedroom apartments, and six two-bedroom apartments" / "Echo Realty wants to build six stories in an area where zoning rules permit only three." / "goes before the city's zoning board of adjustment on August 10. If the ZBA grants the needed variances, the project would next move to the Planning Commission."

**WESA 2023-11-07** (https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2023-11-07/development-plans-for-the-former-bloomfield-shursave-hit-a-snag), as summarized by our fetch tool (not verbatim except where quoted): LNC district, inside the IZ overlay; relief sought was a height variance (six stories vs. three) and a residential-compatibility-standards variance; the ZBA said height "is policy-making and for the governing body to decide" and "the variances requested are not the minimum that would afford relief"; a special exception for the grocery use was granted.

**NEXTpittsburgh 2023-11-07** (https://nextpittsburgh.com/city-design/bloomfield-development-plans-stalled-after-zoning-variance-is-denied/): "The grocery store would be built along Howley Street with five stories of apartments. Retail shops would be located near Ella Street with four stories of apartments above them. Indoor and underground parking was also planned." / "The five-to-six-story complex required a variance because the current site is zoned for only three stories or 45 feet."

**WESA 2023-11-21** (https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2023-11-21/after-high-profile-projects-rejected-calls-for-zoning-reform-mount-in-pittsburgh): "Echo said the financing required a structure that would be six stories tall – twice what is allowed" / "the board found that it didn't have the power to alter restrictions on building heights … 'is policy-making and for the governing body to decide.'" The City's chief economic development officer: "There is only but so far that the zoning board can go … It's law."

**WESA 2023-12-06** (https://www.wesanews.org/development-transportation/2023-12-06/bloomfield-shursave-developer-zoning-appeal-housing), as summarized by our fetch tool: appeal filed in the Allegheny County Court of Common Pleas by the project entity, arguing the site's "topography and soil conditions create uniquely challenging and expensive limitations".

**NEXTpittsburgh 2024-07-24** (https://nextpittsburgh.com/city-design/affordable-housing-plans-in-bloomfield-scrapped/): "Echo Realty dropped plans … The company recently transferred the site to its affiliate, Giant Eagle" / "Echo's original redevelopment proposal in 2021 was for a four-story complex with 190 apartments." / "In 2020, Echo Realty purchased the site for nearly $6 million" / "The Bloomfield Development Corp. says no housing will be added at this time."
