# Pro-Housing Pittsburgh, "You Can't Build That Here" (YCBTH): zoning-analysis excerpts

- **Publisher:** Pro-Housing Pittsburgh (advocacy group; blog authors are volunteers, cited here by role only)
- **Index page:** https://www.prohousingpgh.org/ycbth
- **Retrieved:** 2026-09-27, via each post's Squarespace JSON (`<post-url>?format=json`), body text extracted
- **Why saved:** these are the only analyses we hold that walk a named Pittsburgh parcel through the code rule by rule. They are advocacy posts, so every rule value below is a **lead, not code** (README domain rule). The posts date from 2023-10 to 2026-06, which means most lot-size figures **predate Ord. 10-2025 (eff. 5-7-2025)**.

Short verbatim excerpts, one per entry, holding the load-bearing claim. Ellipses mark cuts.

1. **Woodlawn Apartments, 5176 Margaret Morrison St (2023-10-25).** "The lot is small, 5,529 square feet … zoned R2-L … It is parcel 53-C-93 … requires a minimum lot size of 3,000 square feet per unit, requires setbacks of 30 feet from the front and back of the lot, 30 feet from the exterior side, 5 feet from the interior side, and a height maximum of 3 stories or 40 feet … the largest building they could build by right would be a single family three story home of ~2,250 square feet, instead of the four story, 11,982 square foot apartment building"
2. **732-734 S Millvale Ave (2023-11-08).** "24 apartments … roughly 32,000 square feet … The lot it sits on is 11,700 square feet … zoned R2-M … parcel 51-J-37 … requires minimum lot sizes of 3,200 square feet per lot and a minimum of 1,800 square feet per unit … it could at most be replaced by 3 duplexes on 3 lots … a grand total of 4,500 square feet of floor space where there currently exists 32,000"
3. **21 Lanark St (2023-11-22).** "parcel 21-C-213, is 20 ft by 90 ft - 1,800 square feet … R1D-H … minimum front, rear, and exterior side setbacks of 15 ft, and interior side setbacks of 5 ft … The largest house that could be built by right would be 10 feet wide by 60 feet long". Case history: nonprofit applied (June 2022) for 8 affordable houses on Lanark St; ZBA granted variances (March 2023); neighbors sued (April 2023); Common Pleas ruled (Sept 2023) the variances should not have been granted; WESA quote: about "$120,000 in costs … and at least a year of additional time".
4. **The Eaglemoor, 1137 N Highland Ave (2023-12-06).** "21 apartments … approximately 30,000 square feet … parcel 82-M-167, is 16,543 square feet … zoned R1D-L … requires minimum lot sizes of 5,000 square feet, and only one housing unit can be built per lot … the maximum … would be 3 single family houses"
5. **Hillsboro Plaza, 612 Hillsboro St (2023-12-20).** "8 stories tall and has 73 units … 54,800 square feet … 32,287 square foot lot … parcel 42-S-36 is zoned LNC … maximum floor area ratio of 2:1, but Hillsboro Plaza has a 1.69:1 ratio … housing for the elderly … Parking Demand Analysis (914.02.B) … LNC districts have a maximum height of 45 ft, not to exceed 3 stories"
6. **Palm Garden Apartments, 873 Boggs Ave (2024-01-03).** "54 studio and 1 bedroom apartments … 98,000 square feet … 15-N-75, is zoned P (Parks District) … only Single Family Residential are permitted by right in Parks Districts … Even if multi-unit apartment buildings were allowed by right … prohibited because it is more than 3 stories tall"
7. **Carson Towers, 2117 E Carson St (2024-01-17).** "two parcels, 12-F-372 and 12-F-365 … 42,240 square feet … LNC … Floor Area ratio to a maximum of 2:1 … easily surpassing a 4:1 … height maximum of 45 feet … Site Plan Review … Section 922.04.E … $750 fee … up to 30 day delay … less than 50 feet away from a R1A zone … height … capped at 40 feet or 3 stories" (916.02.B.1)
8. **The School House Apartments, 500 Tripoli St (2024-01-31).** "77 units … roughly 120,000 square feet … parcel number 23-M-196, is 1.1 acres or roughly 48,000 square feet, and is zoned R1A-VH … minimum lot sizes of 1,200 square feet and only a single residence may be built per lot … height maximum of 40 feet or 3 stories … at most 40 units could be built on this lot today"
9. **Park View Three, 7 N Commons (2024-02-14).** "11 stories … approximately 190,000 square feet, and has ~210 units … 23-R-30, is 2 acres or roughly 87,000 square feet, and is zoned UNC … UNC limits heights to 3 stories (not to exceed 45 feet), unless within 1,500 feet of a 'Major transit facility' … 4 stories (not to exceed 60 feet), or if a Special Exception is granted … 8 stories (not to exceed 85 feet) … Floor Area Ratio is limited to 3:1 or 4:1 … parking minimums require 1 spot for every unit"
10. **1703 Broadway Ave (2024-02-28).** "12 unit, 3 story apartment building … parcel 35-K-192 - which is 9,020 square feet and is zoned … R1D-H … multifamily in a Single Unit Residential … attached … in a Detached zone … lacks the 15 ft front and rear setbacks … lacks the 12 units of off street parking … it would only fit 5 single family homes"
11. **Dover Gables, 555-575 S Negley Ave (2024-04-25).** Northern half lots (e.g. 51-M-343) "R1A-VH"; southern lot "84-J-59 … RM-M". "violates the 25 foot minimum rear setback … no more than fifteen feet … minimum lot size for a parcel containing twenty units is 1,800 square feet per unit, or 36,000 … it has only 30,719 square feet … requires twenty off-street parking spaces … Motheral owns only eight spots"
12. **Park View Flats, 1401 N St Clair (2024-07-03).** "16 two bedroom apartments … zoned R2 - L … you cannot build more than two apartments on it … 7450 square foot parcel … low density designation requires a minimum lot size of 5000 square feet … front, rear, and exterior setbacks of 30 feet … height restriction of 40 ft, not to exceed 3 stories"
13. **August Wilson House, 1727 Bedford Ave (2024-07-17).** "current parcel is 9-S-36 and the zoning is RM-M … requires minimum lot sizes of 3,200 square feet, front setbacks of 25 feet, and interior side yard setbacks of 10 feet … grocery stores are not a permitted use in RM-M … 1,800 square feet per unit … On the 1890 dimensions of that lot [24 x 115.3 ft, 2,767 sf], no building could be built"
14. **3525 Beechwood Blvd (2024-07-31).** "fourplex … parcel number 88-B-44 … R2-M … R2 zoning only allows up to a duplex … the lot is only 5,007 square feet … 1,800 square feet per unit … 30 foot front yard setback, this building has a maximum of 20 foot … 33 ft wide building is on a 40 ft wide lot … 1 parking space per unit"
15. **H.G. Imhoff House, 100 N Linden Ave (2024-12-15).** "9,164 square foot lot … R1D-L … permits only single-family detached homes … ten-unit … minimum lot size of 3,000 square feet per dwelling unit … ten off-street parking spaces … minimum rear setback of 30 feet"
16. **Clark Building, 717 Liberty Ave (2026-04-26).** "zoned 'GT-C' … Floor Area Ratio (FAR) of no more than 10 … parcel 1-D-66, has a lot size of 16,181 square feet … building square footage of 291,000 … FAR of >17". Also: "Just a few weeks ago, the Zoning Board denied a request for a variance to have a paltry FAR of 10 in the Strip District, at the corner of 17th and Penn".
17. **"You Can Build That, Just Not Here" (2026-06-09), The Waterfront (Homestead / West Homestead, outside the City).** "250 units … three acres … Waterfront Development District … multifamily … by right … Parking minimums: 1 space per bedroom or 1.5 spaces per unit, whichever is lower … maximum of 7 stories or 84 feet … Setbacks: a minimum of 30 feet from public roads and railways, 25 feet from the river, and 0 feet on all other sides … nearest equivalent is probably RM-H"
