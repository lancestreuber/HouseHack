# URA gap-finance program rules: Rental Gap Program (RGP), For-Sale Development Program (FSDP), and the 2026 HOF Annual Allocation Plan. Excerpts

Accessed 2026-09-27. We extracted text with pdftotext. The RGP 2024 PDF has a broken font encoding, so we read it as page images instead. Staff and principals are omitted.

## 1. RGP Guidelines, "Last Updated August 2024" (current)
The file is named "Amended August 2024 - Correct 12.2025". It is linked from https://www.ura.org/pages/rental-gap-program.
https://www.ura.org/media/W1siZiIsIjIwMjUvMTIvMDMvM2wwcDl1MnRpbF9SZW50YWxfR2FwX1Byb2dyYW1fR3VpZGVsaW5lc19BbWVuZGVkX0F1Z3VzdF8yMDI0X0NvcnJlY3RfMTIuMjAyNS5wZGYiXV0/Rental%20Gap%20Program%20Guidelines_Amended%20August%202024%20-%20Correct%2012.2025.pdf

§VI.A Maximum Loan Amounts (transcribed from the page image):

| Unit Affordability | Maximum Loan |
|---|---|
| 30% AMI | $75,000 per unit |
| 50% AMI | $50,000 per unit |
| 60% AMI | $35,000 per unit |
| Additional funding for services | $10,000 per unit, up to $200,000 (HOF sourced only) |
| Maximum RGP funding per project | $2,000,000 (typically $1,500,000 - $1,750,000) |

Other terms, verbatim or close paraphrase:
- §IV.6 "The property must contain at least 4 units."
- §IV.8 minimum affordability: for 4–40 units, 4 affordable units; for 41+ units, "At least 10% of all units must be affordable". Units may be any mix of ≤30%, ≤50% or ≤60% AMI.
- §VI.B "The borrower is required to provide equity equal to or exceeding 10 percent of the total development cost." This can be waived.
- §VI.C.2 affordability "for at least forty (40) years. Priority will be given to projects that have permanent affordability (99 years)."
- §VI.C.3 "The gross rent for units to be occupied by households who earn at or below 60% AMI may not exceed 30% of 50% AMI." (The 50% and 30% tiers are capped at 30% of 50% AMI and 30% of 30% AMI.)
- §VI.E term: no longer than the first mortgage, or 30 years from completion, whichever is longer.
- §VI.F.4 cash sweep: the URA may seek pre-payment "in the event that the Borrower's Net Operating Income (NOI) for the preceding year exceeds 130% of scheduled debt service payments."
- §VI.F.5 for LIHTC or HUD 811/202 projects, the loan "may continue to be deferred until the maturity date".
- §VI.H.4.c new construction: "A minimum of 10% of units shall be accessible to individuals with mobility impairments and a minimum of 4% shall be accessible to individuals with sensory impairments."
- §III.2 "HOF funded developments require a Non-Profit applicant in the development team."

**Prior version, for contrast.** The RGP Guidelines dated 7/9/2020 (https://www.ura.org/media/W1siZiIsIjIwMjAvMTAvMDgvNzUxbW83bmNkMV9SR1BfUHJvZ3JhbV9HdWlkZWxpbmVzX0p1bHlfMjAyMC5wZGYiXV0/RGP%20Program%20Guidelines%20-%20July%202020.pdf) set these caps: 30% AMI $60,000/unit; 50% AMI $30,000/unit; 60% AMI $25,000/unit; per project "$1,250,000 (typically $400,000 - $600,000)".

**Contradiction in URA's own record.** The minutes of the URA Board meeting of June 2025 (item 6a, El Court) describe the waiver as exceeding "the maximum loan amount of $30,000 per unit affordable at or below 60% of AMI, $50,000 per unit affordable at or below 50% of AMI, and $75,000 per unit affordable at or below 30% of AMI". **That is $30k at 60% AMI, while the August 2024 guidelines say $35k.** The same minutes (Res. 75) waive "the maximum loan amount of $2,000,000" for the May Building, which received $3,000,000.

## 2. FSDP Guidelines, dated 09.2024
Linked from https://www.ura.org/pages/for-sale-development-program.
https://www.ura.org/media/W1siZiIsIjIwMjQvMDkvMDQvOHdsZGlzcmkzaF9FeGhpYml0X0FfRlNEUF9HdWlkZWxpbmVzXzkuMjAyNC5wZGYiXV0/Exhibit%20A-%20FSDP%20Guidelines%209.2024.pdf

Verbatim:
> "Upon project completion, properties financed under FSDP must be sold to owner-occupants who are at or below 80% of the Area Median Income (AMI)."

> "Dependent on the availability of funding sources (i.e. funds other than HOF, CDBG, and HOME) that allow for higher levels of affordability, properties financed under FSDP may be sold to owner-occupants who are between 80% and 115% of the AMI."

> "The maximum amount of FSDP funds per rehabbed unit cannot exceed $100,000.00."

> "The maximum amount of FSDP funds per newly constructed unit cannot exceed $130,000.00."

> "If FSDP funds are conveyed in the form of a construction loan, the maximum loan amount will be limited to 30% of the post-appraised value of the project for for-profit developers."

> "If FSDP funds are conveyed in the form of a grant, the maximum grant amount cannot exceed the difference between the total development costs and the total proposed sales price(s) for the project unit(s)."

Also:
- Predevelopment loans are capped at the lesser of 50% of the approved predevelopment budget, $33,000 per unit, or $100,000 for multi-property projects.
- Construction loans to for-profit developers carry 2–4% interest. Loans to non-profits carry 0%.
- The maximum term is 18 months, extendable by 6–12 months.
- Eligible types: "detached, semi-detached and townhouse units."
- Awards above $250,000 need URA Board approval.
