## Land Bank minutes (text, paraphrased; summaries of public comment)

- **Feb 14, 2025.** A pastor said the congregation had maintained an adjacent parcel for about 15 years "while navigating between the city and URA for acquisition". A separate commenter owns a condemned property with significant back taxes that could not be sold. https://pghlandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/20250214_BOARD-MEETING_MINUTES.docx.pdf
- **Jul 11, 2025.** An applicant family has been "trying to acquire this land since 2018". The minutes' resolutions also budget "quieting the title … at a cost not to exceed $20,000". https://pghlandbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/20250711_BOARD-MEETING_MINUTES-1.pdf
- **2025 to 2026.** Nearly every acquisition resolution includes title quieting as a prep cost, recurring through November 2025 and February, March and June 2026. Index: https://pghlandbank.org/agendas-and-minutes-2/
