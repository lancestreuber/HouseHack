- URL: https://www.rsmeans.com/media/wysiwyg/quarterly_updates/2021-CCI-LocationFactors-V2.pdf. The file is a 76-page PDF that RSMeans published free of charge. We extracted its text with pdftotext.
- Accessed 2026-09-27. We tried the same URL pattern for the years 2022 through 2026. Every one returned a placeholder image, not a PDF. **The 2021 file is the newest public copy we found.** Current RSMeans data is paid.
- Also: the Q2 2023 change notice, https://www.rsmeans.com/media/wysiwyg/quarterly_updates/Q2-2023-ChangeNotice.pdf. This notice gives quarter-over-quarter changes, not index levels.

The PDF describes how to use the factors, verbatim:
> "Costs shown in Residential Costs with RSMeans data are based on national averages for materials and installation. To adjust these costs to a specific location, simply multiply the base cost by the factor for that city."
