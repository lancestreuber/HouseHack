# PLI Revised Private Demolition Requirements (issued 4/27/2021): excerpts

- **Publisher:** City of Pittsburgh, Department of Permits, Licenses and Inspections (PLI)
- **URL:** https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/pli/documents/pli-bulletins/14153_revised_private_demo_requirements.pdf
- **Document date:** Issued 4/27/21 (page footers 4/27/2021)
- **Accessed:** 2026-09-27. Plain curl gets an Akamai "Access Denied" (HTTP 403). The PDF was retrieved through a web-fetch tool and converted with `pdftotext -layout`.
- **Scope:** This covers **private** demolition permits. City-funded demolitions are bid separately; their scope text appears in each permit's `work_description` in WPRDC `pli-permits` (see sweep r9).
- The document references "PLI's current demolition specifications", which were **not** found or read.

## Verbatim excerpts

§1(e)(iii) Special Conditions: Basement Walls
> "Demolition of a structure with a shared basement wall or basement wall that abuts the basement wall of adjacent structure. E/A to confirm basement wall will remain stable under new loading condition (serving as retaining wall) and specify how the wall will be waterproofed to prevent moisture migration to adjacent structure."

§1(e)(iv) Site Conditions
> "Demolition of a structure located on a parcel in one of the following Department of City Planning overlay districts: (1) Landslide prone. … (2) Steep Slope. … (3) Undermined Area. EA to evaluate site conditions and demolition operations. Report to recommend how to maintain site stability during and post demolition, including mine grouting, as applicable. Application to include PA Department of Environmental Protection 'Coal Status Report'."

§3(a) Specifications
> "Perform work in compliance with PLI's current demolition specifications and approved demo narrative and reports, as applicable."

§3(b)–(c) Inspections
> "Request the following required inspections: pre-construction, void, and final; no exceptions will be granted."
> "The pre-construction meeting allows the permit holder to confirm existing conditions, presence of a basement; and all required protection measures with Inspector prior to the start of demolition operations."

§3(d) Void Inspection
> "The following are required for a successful void inspection and authorization to backfill: i) Documentation of ACHD and water utility agency approval of termination of water and sewer service lines. ii) Void exposed to verify that basement slabs and building materials are broken into pieces 24 inches or less. iii) Waterproofing of basement walls, if required."

§3(e) Final Inspection
> "i) Copies of receipts from disposal agency identifying job-site location and the disposal location(s) and date(s).
> ii) All voids shall be backfilled with clean fill per PA Department of Environmental Protection 'Safe Fill Regulations' and shall exclude wood, roots, grass, coal, and carbonaceous shale.
> iii) Site shall be returned to natural grade, slope away from adjoining properties/structures, and level with sidewalk.
> iv) The top of the grading to be a minimum of top 4" of topsoil.
> v) The property is to be seeded and covered with straw at completion."

## Our reading (inference, not the source's words)
- The text requires the basement **slab** to be broken to pieces of 24 inches or less and the void to be backfilled. It does **not** require the basement or foundation **walls** to be removed. Where a wall abuts a neighbour, it is expected to stay and act as a retaining wall.
- Whether broken slab and "building materials" may stay in the void is ambiguous. The void inspection checks that they are broken up, and the final inspection asks for disposal receipts.
- Compaction is not specified (no Proctor or lift thickness). A builder should expect a buried foundation and uncompacted or unknown fill.
