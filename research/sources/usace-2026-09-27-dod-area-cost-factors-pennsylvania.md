## Method and caveats (verbatim, 2024)
> "the Tri-Service Cost Engineering ACF software program evaluates the local costs for a United States (US) market basket of 8 labor crafts, 18 construction materials, and 4 equipment items."

> "There is no easy correlation between the current ACF and the previous ACF for a specific location. No common benchmark exists because both the Base City average and the relationships between cities may change from year to year."

> "These factors are NOT intended for use to develop and/or modify individual facilities Sustainment Costs, detailed Quantity-Take-offs, Unit Price Book (UPB) line items, Commercial cost data, or User Generated unit costs."
