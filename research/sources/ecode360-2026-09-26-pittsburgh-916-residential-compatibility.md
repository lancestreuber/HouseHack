## §916.01.B Applicability (verbatim)

> 1. Building Height and Setback Standards. The Building Height and Setback regulations of the Residential Compatibility Standards shall apply to all non-residential development in residential zoning districts, and all development in RM-M, RM-H, RM-VH and all non-residential base zoning districts when such development is located:
> (a) Across the street from or adjacent to a property that is zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, or H; or
> (b) Within one hundred (100) feet of a property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, or H.
>
> 2. Other Standards. The Screening, Site Design, Operating Hours, Noise, Lighting and Odor Regulations of the Residential Compatibility Standards shall apply to all non-residential development in residential zoning districts, and to all development in all non-residential base zoning districts when such development is located:
> (a) Across the street from or adjacent to a property that is zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R5, RM, or H; or
> (b) Within two hundred (200) feet of a property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, RM, or H.

(The code prints "R5" in 2(a). This looks like a typo for R3. It is quoted as printed.)

## §916.02.B Building Heights (verbatim)

> 1. Within 50 Feet of Residential. Structures or portions of structures shall not exceed forty (40) feet or three (3) stories in height when located within fifty (50) feet of property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, or H.
> 2. 51 to 100 Feet from Residential. Structures or portions of structures shall not exceed fifty (50) feet or four (4) stories in height when located fifty-one (51) to one hundred (100) feet from property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, or H.
> 3. More Than 100 Feet from Residential. No compatibility-related height restrictions shall be imposed on structures located more than one hundred (100) feet from property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, or H.

## §916.02.A Setbacks (summary, not verbatim)
Nine cases apply, keyed to which side of the subject lot faces which side of the "R" lot. The recurring rules are:
- Interior side yard abutting an R lot: at least 15 ft.
- Rear yard: the greater of the abutting R district's rear (or front, or street-side) setback and 15 ft.
- Front or street-side setback: matches the abutting R district's setback "for the first fifty (50) feet adjacent to the Residential zoning lot".

## §916.04 Site design (verbatim, A–C)
> A. No swimming pool, tennis court, ball field, or playground area shall be permitted within fifty (50) feet of property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, RM, or H.
> B. Dumpsters and refuse receptacles shall be located a minimum of thirty (30) feet from any property zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, RM, or H.
> C. Parking and loading shall not be located within fifteen (15) feet of properties zoned R1D, R1A, R2, R3, RM, or H. [...]

## Other sections (summary)
- §916.03: screening per Ch. 918.
- §916.05: no garbage collection or loading from 10pm to 6am.
- §916.06: noise limit of 45 dB(A) at night and 55 dB(A) in the day, at the R lot line.
- §916.07: lighting of 0.4 fc or less, 3 ft inside the R lot line.
- §916.08: odor.
- §916.09: ZBA special exception may waive the standards. A height waiver is available only if a taller intervening structure exists.
